Players

Goals, assists and clean sheets for players in the top five leagues, 2012/13 to 2025/26.

Type at least two letters. Accents count, so search Ødegaard, not Odegaard.

    Player leaders

    Select a player to see every season. Players who changed club are added together, and the clubs are listed under the name. Assists and clean sheets come from Transfermarkt: a clean sheet means a goalkeeper played 90 minutes and his team conceded no goals.

    Season by season stats

    Seasons run from 2012/13 to 2025/26, so the current season is not included yet. Cups are the FA Cup, Copa del Rey, DFB-Pokal and Coppa Italia, plus the Champions League and Europa League. The EFL Cup and Coupe de France are not in the data.

    For information only, not betting advice. Data from football-data.co.uk and Transfermarkt.
