Twenty years of results

  • More than 38,000 matches, from 2005/06 to today
  • Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1
  • Stored in a PostgreSQL database
  • New results are added automatically, without duplicates

How it works

  • Each team is described by its last five matches: points, goals, shots, corners and days of rest
  • Every number comes only from games played before the match, so the model never sees the result
  • Trained on 33,204 matches before July 2024, then tested only on later games, never on a random mix
  • Logistic regression predicts the outcome. It did as well as random forest and gradient boosting, and it is the easiest to explain
  • Bookmaker odds are only a benchmark. They are never fed into the model

Goals and corners

  • Poisson regression estimates the expected total for each match
  • That total is turned into the chance of going over each line, such as 2.5 goals
  • It beats a plain average on every line, but only by a small margin. Recent form says a little about totals, not a lot

What it can't do

  • It only sees recent results, with no lineups, injuries or transfers
  • It almost never picks a draw, even though draws are about a quarter of results
  • Its home-win chances run a little high, by 1.5 to 3.5 points
